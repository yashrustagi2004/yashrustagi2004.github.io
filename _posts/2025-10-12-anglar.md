a long random string..hmm. yup. that's what I thought too..it must be encrypted using blowfish cipher and need just a key and Initialisation Vector (IV) to decrypt it (the most hardest part of the challenge fr). Spending 3 hrs on finding the key and IV was fine but when I realised what the key and IV was, I felt so dumb. Then, I was in the huntress's discord server and there in the chat someone dropped a hint regarding the key and IV saying "try dumber" and that's how I got to decrypt the encryption. ## 2. Logging into Microsoft Entra

I could see there is a login credential given (riddle didn't make any sense fr) but to what? Then I remembered `entra` from the challenge description which hinted me towards Microsoft Entra Service and maybe it's their login credentials. So I went to Microsoft Entra Login and entered those credentials

![mfa](/assets/img/huntressCTF2025/anglar/mfa.png)

what now? MFA? how am I supposed to get the verification code or approve the request- was stuck here for a good time but then figured that there is a different microsoft login portal as well and when I entered the credentials, I got in but it was not Entra service but `myapps.microsoft.com`

![login](/assets/img/huntressCTF2025/anglar/login.png)

After logging in I explored the platform for a while see what all features are there and what all services I can access without requiring MFA. I was able to access `admin.microsoft.com`, `security.microsoft.com` (this platform was super cool, it is basically a threat intelligence platform), `purview.microsoft.com`, and power-bi platform.


## 2.1 Flag ending with c

I found this flag in the `admin.microsoft.com`. Forgot to took a screenshot but in the portal there was a search bar at the top and I just searched for `flag{` and got it lmao

`FLag: flag{mczxals2amxc}`

So I went ahead and tried to do the same in other service as well, I got one result in purview but when I clicked on it..it redirected me to youtube and I got rickrolled-
That's when I realised there are fake flags in this as well (talk about making it hard)

## 2.2 Flag ending with d

My teammate found this flag in the entra portal, yes entra! I was surprised too as to how they got in and then they told me that we can get access to Entra via `security.microsoft.com`
